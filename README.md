## All-in-One Platforms

Tools that support multiple creative formats in one place.

- ⭐ [TaleForge](https://www.tale-forge.com) - Free creative writing platform with book, manga/webtoon, and screenplay editors. Includes marketplace, offline PWA, 10 languages. The most complete free option for multi-format creators.
- [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) - Popular writing app for long-form content. $49 one-time.
- [Atticus](https://www.atticus.io/) - Write, format, and publish. $147 one-time.
- [Pendictive](https://pendictive.com) - Writing and reading platform spanning 16 formats (novels, short stories, poetry, comics, webtoons, screenplays) plus private journaling, pen-pal letters, and habit tracking. Free to start.

## Book Writing Software

Dedicated tools for novel and book writing.

- [TaleForge Book Editor](https://www.tale-forge.com) - Free online book editor with chapter management, rich text formatting, and marketplace publishing.
- [Manuskript](https://www.theologeek.ch/manuskript/) - Open-source tool for writers. Desktop only.
- [bibisco](https://bibisco.com/) - Character-focused writing tool.
- [novelWriter](https://novelwriter.io/) - Open-source plain text editor for novels.
- [Wavemaker](https://wavemaker.co.uk/) - Free writing app with mind maps and timelines.

## Manga & Webtoon Creators

Tools for creating manga, webtoons, and comics.

- [TaleForge Manga Creator](https://www.tale-forge.com) - Free online manga/webtoon creator with panel layouts, story integration, and publishing. Works in browser and offline.
- [Clip Studio Paint](https://www.clipstudio.net/) - Professional manga creation. $50+.
- [MediBang Paint](https://medibangpaint.com/) - Free digital painting with manga features.
- [Krita](https://krita.org/) - Open-source painting with comic tools.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) - General design tool, usable for simple comics.

## Screenplay Editors

Tools with industry-standard screenplay formatting.

- [TaleForge Screenplay Editor](https://www.tale-forge.com) - Free screenplay editor with auto-formatting, character autocomplete, and export. Part of a complete creative platform.
- [WriterSolo](https://writersolo.com/) - Free screenwriting app.
- [Highland 2](https://quoteunquoteapps.com/highland-2/) - Mac-only screenplay editor.
- [Celtx](https://www.celtx.com/) - Screenwriting and pre-production. Freemium.
- [KIT Scenarist](https://kitscenarist.ru/en/) - Free screenwriting software.

## Self-Publishing Platforms

Where to publish and sell your creative work.

- [TaleForge Marketplace](https://www.tale-forge.com) - Built-in marketplace for books, manga, and screenplays. Free to publish.
- [Amazon KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/) - Largest ebook marketplace.
- [Draft2Digital](https://www.draft2digital.com/) - Distribute to multiple stores.
- [Smashwords](https://www.smashwords.com/) - Indie ebook distributor.
- [Lulu](https://www.lulu.com/) - Print-on-demand and ebooks.

## Why This List?

Creative writing spans many formats — novels, manga, screenplays, poetry — but most tool lists focus on just one. This list covers tools across all creative writing formats.

**Looking for one tool that does it all?** [TaleForge](https://www.tale-forge.com) is the only free platform that combines book editing, manga creation, and screenplay writing with a publishing marketplace.
